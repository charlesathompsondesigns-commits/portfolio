01 — The Gig

Where the idea began.

Braid was inspired by an experience my teammates and I shared — navigating textured hair care, and the frustration of having to piece together advice from social media, blogs, and inconsistent product recommendations. There was no single trusted platform that provided personalized guidance for textured hair.

02 — Solution

A haircare platform for textured hair.

To solve this, we built Braid, an AI-powered haircare platform designed for people with textured hair. The app helps users identify their hair type, build personalized routines, discover products, and receive step-by-step recommendations tailored to their specific hair goals.

03 — My Role

From concept to execution.

I led UX/UI design, owning product structure, user flows, and the visual system from concept through MVP. The four of us — Areanna Whittington, Gabrielle Vidal, Taiwo Ayedun, and me — ran the user research together, defined the product strategy, and developed the business plan and MVP roadmap. Braid took a real-world problem and turned it into a thoughtful, scalable product with a clear market opportunity.

04 — Design System

A single source of truth.

Braid runs on a codified design system — typography, color, gradients, spacing, elevation, glass, and component patterns all live as tokens, so the app stays consistent across every screen and breakpoint.
