User Interviews & Experience Validation

We run iterative, mixed-method research to reduce risk at each stage of development: surveys help us quickly quantify needs and prioritize what to build, one-on-one interviews uncover the why behind behaviors and language we design around, and live demo events validate usability and product-market clarity in real contexts, directly informing design decisions and roadmap tradeoffs.
