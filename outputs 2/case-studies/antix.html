Operations Dashboard

I built the ANTIX Ops Board and custom Notion system to centralize every task, streamline cross team communication, and keep the entire production aligned from planning to show day.

I not only designed and produced all of the environmental signs for ANTIX, but I also set up the full tap to pay system for our volunteers. With almost no prior experience, I learned the tools quickly, configured every device, and ensured the system was secure and ready for show day. Balancing both signage production and payment operations allowed our team to stay organized, reduce stress, and keep the event running smoothly.
